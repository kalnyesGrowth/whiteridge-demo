Our Story

A Family Farm
Built on Four Generations

White Ridge Farm has stood in Somerville, Virginia since the early 1900s — founded by Lloyd Grove and passed through four generations of the same family on the same land. What started as a diversified operation became one of Virginia's most respected registered Angus programs under Bobby Grove's leadership beginning in 1979.

Bobby built the program from two Angus cow-calf pairs into a nationally recognized seedstock operation, co-founded the celebrated "Headquarters for Hindquarters" bull sale, served as President of the Virginia Angus Association, and was named Virginia BCIA Seedstock Producer of the Year in 2014. He passed in November 2025, leaving behind a legacy that the Grove family continues with pride.

"Most of the time, we just have to figure out how to keep moving forward. This is home."

— Donna Grove, White Ridge Farm

Current Herd Sires

The Bulls Behind
Your Next Calf Crop

Each herd sire at White Ridge is chosen for the total package — not one trait. These bulls represent genetics sourced from the best programs in Virginia and Montana, selected to complement each other across the whole herd.

Calving Ease · Volume · Flesh
McCumber Radar 1105

Stout, wide-based bull with big volume and easy fleshing traits. Calving ease genetics from McCumber Angus (Rolette, ND). Sired out of the Armour line — a dam that's a Pathfinder daughter of Rito Rolette.

Heifer Safe · Maternal · Efficiency
Ginger Hill RattleOn 182

Heifer-friendly, low birthweight sire that produces attractive and useful progeny. Combines high production with forage efficiency. His daughters are exceptional — co-owned with Ginger Hill.

No-Fault · Growth · Maternal Strength
McCumber Palmer 295

Sired by OCC Edge of Glory from a Tremendous dam. Produces no-fault, balanced trait, low-input cattle with strong growth and eye appeal. Maternal strength is bred in deep.

Muscle · Eye Appeal · Daughters
Skyline Resolve 307

First offering from Skyline Angus (Stevensville, MT). Coleman Resolve × Cole Creek Cedar Ridge. Calves noted for muscle development and eye appeal — co-owned with C Squared Cattle.

Length · Width · Performance
Coleman Keen 717

From Coleman Angus (Charlo, MT) — a nationally respected maternal herd. Tremendous length, base width, volume, muscle, and performance. Pedigree includes EXT, Rito 707, and Charlo. Sires excellent performance in structurally sound, heavy-muscled cattle.

Heifer Safe · Muscle · Early Shedding
Ginger Hill Black Bore 616

Deemed "most outstanding sire" by breeder Brooke Miller. Heifer-safe, yet sires excellent muscle, growth, and maternal value. Progeny demonstrate early hair shedding. Dam: 10-year-old Pathfinder cow, 107 WWR on 8 calves.
